# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

### Phase 1: Launch (Week 1)
* **Product Hunt**: Launch as a free "AI Savings Calculator" to capture top-of-funnel leads.
* **LinkedIn Outreach**: Post the "Annual Recovery" results to niche groups (SaaS Founders, Engineering Managers).

### Phase 2: Engagement (Week 2-4)
* **Cold Outreach**: Use the Supabase lead list to send personalized "Migration Roadmaps" to users who identified >$5,000 in leakage.
* **Content Marketing**: Publish a report on "The State of AI Spend in 2026" based on anonymized audit data.

### Phase 3: Monetization
* **Affiliate Revenue**: Partner with AI providers to offer "Switch & Save" discounts.
* **Premium Audit**: Offer a deep-dive manual audit for Enterprise clients.
